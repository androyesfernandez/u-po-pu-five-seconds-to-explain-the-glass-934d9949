A house cat defends itself in the five seconds after knocking a glass off the table: the human left the glass parked on the very edge like a tiny diving board and then expected a cat with paws and curiosity to stroll past it without ever checking the acoustics of the room. No apology, no confession — the knock was the experiment. Running `main.ts` writes the one-sentence defence to `/output/entry.txt`.
